# Result Interpretation Notes

1. P0 may show high utilization while violating the global capacity constraint; therefore utilization alone must not be interpreted as superior efficiency.
2. P1C, P2S, P2D, and P3 are compared primarily after safety feasibility is established.
3. P2D is a simplified dynamic escrow heuristic used for baseline-fairness checking.
4. Normalized latency is a structural service-cost measure, not production latency.
5. The final lifecycle robustness experiment explicitly exercises timeout → reconciliation after lost terminal reports.
